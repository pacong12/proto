# Launching a Token

Creating your own token on Proto takes less than two minutes and requires zero coding or blockchain development experience.

---

## Step-by-Step Launch Guide

### 1. Connect Your Wallet

Navigate to [%APP_HOST%](%APP_URL%) and click **Connect Wallet** in the top navigation bar. Make sure your wallet is connected to **Robinhood Chain** (Chain ID: 4663).

### 2. Go to the Create Page

Click **Create** or navigate directly to `/launchpad/create`.

### 3. Fill in Token Details

Enter the essential information for your token:

- **Token Name**: The full name (e.g., `Robin Gold`).
- **Ticker Symbol**: 3 to 6 uppercase letters (e.g., `GOLD`).
- **Description**: A short introduction describing your token, purpose, or community vision.
- **Token Logo**: Upload an image file (PNG, JPG, SVG, or WebP). Proto automatically compresses your picture and securely pins it to IPFS.

### 4. Add Community Links (Optional)

You can include official links so traders can verify and join your project:

- Website URL
- Official X (Twitter) handle
- Community Telegram link

### 5. Initial Buy (Optional)

If you wish to purchase an initial allocation as the creator at the launch price, enter the desired ETH amount.

### 6. Confirm & Deploy

Click **Launch Token** and confirm the transaction in your wallet:

- **Launch Fee**: Fixed at `0.0005 ETH` on Robinhood Chain, or `0 USDC` on Arc Network (free launch with minimum 4.50 USDC pre-buy).
- Once the transaction confirms, your token is live, verifiable, and tradable immediately!

---

## What Happens to Token Supply?

Every token created on Proto has a strictly fixed supply:

- **Total Supply**: Exactly **1,000,000,000 tokens** (1 Billion).
- **Decimals**: 18.
- **No Minting**: No function exists to mint additional tokens after launch.
- **No Pausing**: The token cannot be frozen or blacklisted.
