# Feed, Calls, Memestock & Following

Goton has a social layer next to trading: holders post calls on the tokens they hold, follow callers they trust, and Memestock highlights launches with a thesis and a committed creator.

---

## Calls

A **call** is a short post about a token: your thesis, an optional target market cap and an optional image or chart link.

- **You must hold the token.** The app checks your balance on-chain when you post, and a call needs at least about $1 of the token so the feed is not flooded with empty posts.
- **Every call is signed by your wallet.** The signature proves who wrote it. No transaction is sent and no gas is spent.
- Each call shows the share of supply you held when you posted it, the position's value and, once the price moves, the call's profit or loss.

Post a call from the **Feed** page or with the **Call** button on any token page.

---

## The Feed

- **Tabs**: All Calls, Following (calls from accounts you follow, when your wallet is connected), Targets (calls with a target market cap), Whales (callers holding at least 1% of supply or $500), In Profit and Media.
- **Chain filter**: All Chains, Robinhood or Arc.
- **Alpha Radar**: the tokens with the most calls in the last 24 hours and how many calls each has. It is hidden when there are no calls.
- **Top Movers**: the tokens with the most 24-hour trading volume, then the largest price change.

---

## Following

- Open any profile to see its **Followers** and **Following** counts. Click a count to see the accounts.
- Click **Follow** on another user's profile to see their calls in the feed's Following tab. Following needs a connected wallet and a one-time signature to sign in. No transaction is sent.

---

## Memestock

Explore lists every token. **Memestock** lists only the launches that pass three checks:

1. **A written thesis**: a description of at least a few words.
2. **A committed creator**: the creator bought the token on-chain and has sold no more than half of what they bought.
3. **Ranked by momentum**: traders in the last hour, traders in the last 24 hours and the last hour's buy pressure.

Each card shows the creator's remaining holding as a share of supply, the trader counts and the 1-hour buy share. Memestock is empty until a launch qualifies; every coin is still on Explore.
