# PnL Cards & Sharing

Every position has a shareable **PnL card** — a generated image of your profit and loss on a token that you can post, copy or link. It's the quickest way to show a win (or a lesson) from a trade.

---

## Opening a Card

From the **Portfolio** tab on your profile, or from a token's trade view, use the **Share PnL** action on a position. A card opens showing:

- Your **profit and loss** in USD (e.g. `+$35.23K`), with the **percent** return when there's an investment to measure against.
- The **token** the position is on.
- Goton branding, so the card stands on its own when shared.

The same card also lives at its own shareable page, `goton.fun/pnl/<wallet>/<token>`, so anyone you send it to can open it in a browser.

---

## Share Actions

The card modal offers four ways to share:

| Action         | What it does                                                            |
| :------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Post on X**  | Opens X with the PnL, percent, token and a link to your card pre-filled |
| **Copy image** | Copies the card PNG to your clipboard to paste anywhere                 |
| **Download**   | Saves the card PNG to your device                                       |
| **Copy link**  | Copies the `/pnl/…` page link                                           |

Every link a card produces — the posted tweet and the copied link — carries your **referral code** (`?ref=`), so sharing a win also grows your network. See [Invite Friends & Referrals](/protocol/referrals).

---

## Notes

- The card is rendered from your **indexed on-chain trades**, so it reflects real activity — you can't invent a position.
- A card link is public: anyone who opens it sees that position's PnL for that wallet and token. It does not expose anything beyond what the on-chain history already shows.
- PnL and percent use the position's cost basis; a position with no measurable investment shows the USD amount without a percent.
