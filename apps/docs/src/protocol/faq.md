# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find answers to common questions about trading, launching, and managing tokens on Proto.

---

## General Questions

### What is Proto?

Proto is a decentralized, non-custodial crypto launchpad supporting both **Robinhood Chain** (L2) and **Arc Network** (Circle Stablecoin L1). It allows anyone to launch fixed-supply tokens, trade with live charts, and participate in fair launches with permanently locked liquidity.

### Can a developer rug pull or drain liquidity?

**No.** Liquidity pool positions created on Proto are minted directly into the open-source `LiquidityLocker.sol` smart contract and permanently locked forever. There is no unlock function, backdoor, or admin withdrawal key.

### What wallets are supported?

Proto supports all standard Ethereum-compatible Web3 wallets, including:

- MetaMask
- Coinbase Wallet
- Rabby Wallet
- Rainbow Wallet
- WalletConnect / Reown compatible mobile wallets

## Trading & Swaps

### Why did my trade fail?

Trades usually fail for two reasons:

1. **Slippage Exceeded**: During high volatility, the price changed more than your configured slippage percentage between the time you clicked and block confirmation. Increase slippage in trade settings (e.g. from 1% to 2%).
2. **Insufficient Gas Balance**: Always keep a small balance of native gas token (ETH on Robinhood or USDC on Arc) to pay for network execution fees.

### What is the difference between On Curve and Graduated?

- **On Curve (v2)**: The token is currently trading on the mathematical bonding curve. Price increases as tokens are bought.
- **Graduated**: The token accumulated the required liquidity target (4.2 ETH on Robinhood or 8,400 USDC on Arc) on the curve and migrated permanently into a canonical Uniswap DEX liquidity pool.

### Are there any selling restrictions?

**Never.** Users can sell their tokens at any time. Proto never imposes lockups, selling cooldowns, or transfer freezes on holders.

## Token Creation

### How much does it cost to launch a token?

The protocol deployment fee is **0.0005 ETH** on Robinhood Chain or **1 USDC** on Arc Network. This covers smart contract deployment, token minting, and initial liquidity locking.

### Can more tokens be minted later?

**No.** Every token created on Proto has an immutable, fixed supply of exactly **1,000,000,000 tokens** (1 Billion). The contract has no mint function.

### How do creators earn fees?

Creators automatically accumulate **70% of the 1% trading fees** generated by trades in their token. You can check and claim your accrued fee rewards at any time on your **Profile** page (`/profile`).
