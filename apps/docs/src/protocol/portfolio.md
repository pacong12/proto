# Portfolio Dashboard

Your profile has a **Portfolio** tab that summarises how your trading is going — realised and unrealised profit, a win rate, and every token you currently hold — all computed from your on-chain trades.

---

## The Summary

At the top of the Portfolio tab:

| Metric | Meaning |
| :----- | :------ |
| **Total PnL** | Realised + unrealised profit and loss across your positions, in USD |
| **Realised** | Profit and loss you have already locked in by selling |
| **Unrealised** | Paper profit and loss on tokens you still hold, at the current price |
| **Win rate** | The share of your closed positions that ended in profit |

Gains show green, losses red, so the state of your book is readable at a glance.

---

## Holdings

Below the summary, an **open positions** table lists every token you currently hold:

- **Asset** — the token.
- **Balance** — how much you hold.
- **Price** — the token's current USD price.
- **Value** — your holding's current USD value.
- **Actions** — jump to the token's **trade** page, or open a **PnL card** to share your position (see [Invite Friends & Referrals](/protocol/referrals) for how shared links carry your invite code).

A separate list shows your **closed positions** — tokens you have fully sold — with the result of each.

If you hold nothing yet, the tab points you to explore and trade tokens instead.

---

## Notes

- Everything is derived from your **indexed on-chain trades**, not a manual ledger — connect your wallet and your history populates automatically.
- The activity list is a page of your most recent trades, so a full page does not represent your lifetime total.
- Only you see the share and trade shortcuts on your own profile; visitors see the same performance summary and holdings for any wallet they open.
