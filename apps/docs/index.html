Proto Protocol Overview

Proto is an institutional-grade, non-custodial token launchpad and trading protocol deployed on Robinhood Chain (Chain ID: 4663). Unlike traditional bonding curves, Proto uses a direct-pool Uniswap V3 architecture that eliminates migration downtime, MEV vulnerabilities during transition, and fragmented liquidity.

Key Guarantees

  • Atomic 1-Tx Launch: Token minting, pool creation, liquidity provision, and permanent lock execute in one single transaction.
  • Fixed Supply: Exactly 1,000,000,000 tokens (18 decimals). Minting after launch is permanently impossible.
  • Permanent Liquidity Lock: The full position NFT is locked into LiquidityLocker at creation block.

How Launches Work

Every token launched through Proto trades against WETH in its own dedicated Uniswap V3 pool with a 1% fee tier (10000). Trading happens on the open DEX pool from the very first block.

01. CREATE

Token is deployed with 1B supply and 100% of liquidity goes to Uniswap V3.

02. TRADE

Buys and sells execute on Uniswap V3, generating 1% trading fees.

03. GRADUATE

Once 4.2 ETH is paired in the pool, the token graduates. Trading continues in the same pool.

2-Block Anti-Snipe Guard

To prevent sniper bots and MEV bundles from exhausting initial pool liquidity, Proto enforces hardcoded contract checks during the first 2 blocks post-launch:

Block 0 (Launch Block): Only the creator initial buy is permitted from the pool.
Block 1 to 2: Max buy per transaction is capped at 5.5% of supply (55M tokens), and max wallet balance is capped at 5.0% of supply (50M tokens).
Unrestricted Operations: Selling back to the pool and peer-to-peer wallet transfers are never restricted. After block 2, all limits expire permanently.

Fees and Fee Split (70/30)

All trading fees (WETH and Token) generated by the locked Uniswap V3 position accrue in the LiquidityLocker contract.

CREATOR SHARE

70%

Claimable anytime by creator or redirected to token holders.

PROTOCOL SHARE

30%

80% directed to automated TWAP buyback & burn.
